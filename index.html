The exchange

A real problem for a real result.

The rules

One problem. Up to 14 days. Clearly scoped.

The idea is simple: choose one ML problem that can be meaningfully investigated within two weeks.

It can be a research question, an experiment, a prototype, a model improvement, or a small engineering task connected to a real product.

Before we start, we'll agree on the question, the available data or resources, and what a useful result would look like.

The goal isn't necessarily to finish everything in 14 days — it's to make meaningful progress, test the idea, and leave you with something useful.

Data & confidentiality

Open data — ideal for experiments that can be shared publicly.

Anonymized or synthetic data — if the original data can't leave your organization.

Confidential data under NDA — when appropriate.

I'm comfortable with your organization retaining ownership of any work created specifically for the project.

For confidential projects, I'd only ask — where possible — for permission to mention the collaboration and describe my contribution in general, non-confidential terms in my CV.
