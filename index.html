The rules

One problem. Up to 14 days.

Well-scoped. No strings attached.

This is meant for small R&D questions and experiments your team hasn't had time to investigate — not ongoing unpaid engineering work or production-critical development.

The goal is to answer one useful question and learn something from the process.

Data & confidentiality

The problem can use open data, anonymized or synthetic data, or confidential data under NDA when appropriate.

Any work I create specifically for your organization can remain yours.

For confidential projects, I'd only ask — where possible — for permission to mention the collaboration and describe my contribution in general, non-confidential terms in my CV.
